# Patent Proposal-AI Medical Model for clinical Reasoning simulation 
# https://mabualsaud.github.io/Patent-Proposal-Ai-Medical-Model/

## 🧠Research and Patent Proposal:

### Research conducted to address the current issues in medical LLMs that prevent their safe integration into healthcare organizations, including the potential hazards to patient safety from medical outputs containing hallucinations.

### The research examines the main root causes of hallucinations and how it helps build safe clinical reasoning simulation model integrated with a governance layer, the Clinical Protection Intelligence Protocol (CPIP), and an external auditing process to detect and eliminate hallucinations from medical outputs.
